Hearth

A Discord desktop app with Vencord built in. It's lighter than the official app, keeps Discord away from your system, and is run by its community.

Download Source on GitHub

What's different from the official app

  • Vencord built in

    Plugins and themes work from the first launch. There's nothing to install separately or re-apply after an update.

  • Lighter and quicker

    Much lighter and faster than the official Discord app.

  • Discord stays out of your system

    Discord runs without access to your system, which is far better for your privacy.

  • Screenshare with sound on Linux

    Share your screen or a window with audio, on Wayland too, and choose which apps' sound goes out.

  • Game capture on Linux

    Share a fullscreen game straight from the game, so your framerate holds up while you stream. Works with obs-vkcapture.

  • Your Vesktop setup comes along

    On first launch Hearth copies your Vesktop data, so you stay logged in and keep your settings.
